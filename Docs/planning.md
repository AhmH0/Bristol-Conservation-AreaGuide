# Project Proposal

## Business Case
The dataset I decided to pick was the [Conservation areas](https://arcg.is/1OubTP1) in Bristol Dataset. 

### Problem statement
The dataset showcases the conservation areas within Bristol, these are areas of special architectural and historic interest, giving them the need to be preserved. For some people, both tourists and residents oof the city, finding information regarding these historic areas within the city could be difficult, which is why this project will be to create an application that uses the data from "Bristol Open Data" which allows the user to search for these areas of historic importance, view information regarding it and to explore the locations on a map.  

### Business benefits
The application will be mainly for anyone that finds interest in the history of Bristol, this can be both residents and tourists, although it would mainly appeal to tourists, especially since Bristol receives 9 million of them every year. The application as stated before, will make it easier for them to access information regarding important areas of Bristol, making it somewhat of a sightseeing tour guide. 

### Options Considered
The application lets the user search for different conservation areas in the city, while also showcasing information (facts) regarding the said area they choose to visit, it even shows where its located on the map, and allows for easier planning for tourists or anyone interested in visiting them. It saves the user time as they wouldn’t have to do the research on their own – the application compiles all the data in one place. 

### Expected Risks
Some of the risks and challenges I can see coming up during the development of the application would be mainly around making sure it's easy to use, and that there might be a limited amount of information in the dataset regarding some of these conservation areas. However, most of these can be dealt with as I do continuous tests and handle errors properly.
